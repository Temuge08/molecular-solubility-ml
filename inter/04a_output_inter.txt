Interpret:
This output tells us something important: plain Bemis–Murcko scaffold splitting is too coarse for this ESOL dataset to use as our only split strategy.

Two groups dominate:

315 / 1116 = 28.2% have no ring scaffold at all
253 / 1116 = 22.7% collapse to the simple benzene scaffold c1ccccc1

So over half of the dataset belongs to just two scaffold groups.

If we keep the strict split, all 315 acyclic molecules must go into one split, and all 253 benzene-core molecules must go into one split. That's chemically conservative, but it can make the train/validation/test sets very unbalanced and somewhat artificial.
